Your iPhone is the remote for your Mac.

Pouf turns your iPhone into a touchpad, keyboard, media remote and app launcher for your Mac. It works over your home Wi‑Fi, with no account and no cloud.

Everything from the sofa

Three tabs on your iPhone cover what you'd normally get up for.

  • Remote

    A touchpad with left and right click zones, plus Esc, the arrow keys and Return. Tap Type to write on your Mac with the iPhone keyboard.

  • Media

    Play and pause, skip back and forward, change the volume or mute. Full Screen, Esc and Space are one tap away for films and videos.

  • Apps

    Search your Mac's apps and open them, keep favorites, and make groups that open several apps at once, like everything you use in the morning.

  • Keep Mac Awake

    From the Pouf menu on your Mac, keep the screen and the Mac awake for 1, 2 or 4 hours, or until you turn it off.

  • Private by design

    Pair once by scanning a code. Everything is encrypted and stays on your home network, and Face ID keeps the app locked. Privacy policy

Set up in a minute

  1. Install the Mac helper

    Download Pouf Helper, drag it to Applications and give it Accessibility access. Download

  2. Get Pouf on your iPhone

    Pouf for iPhone is coming soon to the App Store.

  3. Scan to pair

    Choose Pair iPhone… in the Pouf menu on your Mac and scan the code with Pouf. After that, your iPhone connects by itself.

What you need

  • A MacmacOS 15 or later
  • An iPhoneiOS 18 or later
  • One Wi‑Fi networkBoth on the same network

The Mac needs to be awake and unlocked while you use Pouf.
