Common questions

My iPhone can't find my Mac
  • Check that Pouf Helper is running: its icon is in the menu bar, and Listening is on in its menu.
  • Put the iPhone and the Mac on the same Wi‑Fi network. Guest networks and some office or hotel networks stop devices from seeing each other.
  • Wake and unlock the Mac. Pouf can't reach a Mac that is asleep or locked.
  • On the iPhone, check that Pouf is turned on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
The pointer doesn't move or keys don't work

Pouf Helper needs Accessibility permission. Click Grant Access in the Pouf menu and turn on Pouf Helper in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

The permission has to be for the copy in Applications. If you replaced or moved the helper and it still doesn't work, remove Pouf Helper from that list with the minus button and grant access again.

Play/pause controls a different app
  • Play/pause, previous and next are media keys. macOS sends them to the app it treats as Now Playing, usually the one that played last.
  • Volume and mute work for the whole Mac.
  • Skipping back and forward, Full Screen, Esc and Space go to the app in front. The top of the Media tab shows that app and can bring another running app to the front.
The pairing code doesn't work

Each code is valid for 2 minutes and works once. Choose Pair iPhone… in the Pouf menu again for a new one. If Listening is off, turn it on first.

How do I remove a paired iPhone?

On the Mac, hover over the iPhone under Devices in the Pouf menu and click the remove button. On the iPhone, use Unpair This Mac in Pouf's settings.

Can I use Pouf away from home?

No. The iPhone and the Mac have to be on the same local network, because Pouf never connects over the internet.
